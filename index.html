Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

SHEIKH SCIENTIFIC is a browser-based scientific calculator. Calculations run on your device. No account is required.

1. Information stored

The app may store up to 40 calculation-history entries and your theme preference in this browser profile's LocalStorage. People using the same browser profile may see the same saved history. The app does not ask you to provide personal profile information. Avoid entering sensitive information in calculations because saved expressions may appear in your local history.

2. How information is used

Locally stored information is used only to display your calculation history and remember your theme. This version has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, login, or app-operated server calls.

3. Information sharing

The app does not send calculations or locally stored data to an app-operated server. Your browser, device, extensions, operating system, and any hosting provider may have their own data practices.

4. Deleting your information

Use “CLEAR HISTORY” to remove calculation history. Use “Clear all local data” to remove history and the saved theme preference. Clearing this site's browser data also removes locally stored information.

5. Security

The app uses a Content Security Policy and includes no third-party scripts or network APIs. LocalStorage is not encrypted; someone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to view it. Public deployments should use HTTPS and appropriate server security headers.

6. Children's privacy

The app does not require registration or request personal information. It is not designed to collect information from children.
